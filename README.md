# TrustGRC AI 360

The Unified AI Governance, Risk & Compliance Platform.

## Overview

TrustGRC AI 360 helps organizations:

- Manage AI Governance
- Assess AI Systems
- Identify Compliance Risks
- Monitor Governance Controls
- Manage Privacy Documentation
- Improve Cybersecurity Readiness
- Support GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and NIST AI RMF compliance

## Technology Stack
